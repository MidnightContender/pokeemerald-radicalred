# Pass 3a: Radical Red moves and abilities

Adds the Radical Red-only moves and abilities marked "Keep" in the Pass 3 picks doc.
Stats follow the RR dex wherever it and the changelog disagree.

| Move | Type | Cat. | Power | Acc. | PP | Effect | Learners |

| Soul Robbery | Psychic | Special | 100 | 100 | 5 | Steals the target's stat boosts first (Spectral Thief) | Mewtwo (Lv 100) |

The new moves are left out of Metronome's pool, which still stops at the Gen 9 moves.

## Abilities

| Ability | Effect | Holders |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Feline Prowess | Doubles Sp. Atk | Meowth, Persian, Alolan Meowth/Persian, Skitty, Delcatty (hidden); Meowstic (slot 2) |
| Striker | Kicking moves deal 1.3x damage | Combusken, Blaziken, Mudsdale (slot 1); Hitmonlee, Tsareena (hidden); Hisuian Decidueye (slot 2) |
| Self Sufficient | Heals 1/16 max HP at the end of each turn | Turtwig, Grotle, Torterra (slot 2) |
| Blazing Soul | Fire moves get +1 priority at full HP | Rapidash, Typhlosion (hidden) |
| Frozen Mist | Shield Dust plus immunity to entry hazard damage | Articuno (hidden, replaces Shield Dust) |

Kicking moves for Striker: Double Kick, Mega Kick, Jump Kick, Rolling Kick, High Jump Kick, Low Kick,
Triple Kick, Blaze Kick, Trop Kick, Thunderous Kick, Triple Axel, Axe Kick, Stomp, Stomping Tantrum,
Low Sweep, Pyro Ball.

## Not in this pass

- Mountaineer and Primal Armor: only RR-only forms have them (Mega Centiskorch/Coalossal, Primal Dialga). Skipped.
- Maybes for later: Draco Barrage (33% recoil), Forbidden Spell, and the medium/hard abilities.
- All RR items.
